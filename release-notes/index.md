# Release notes

## Version 1.0.0 (build 1): first release
*October 2026*

### New
- 🗺️ **Community safety map**: Apple Maps with a red → amber → green overlay. Colour strength shows how many people rated an area.
- ➕ **Rate any spot**: five safety levels, time of day, weekday or weekend, place type, 24 concern and positive tags, alone or with others, first-hand or second-hand, plus an optional note.
- 📍 **Area details**: day vs after-dark scores, how ratings are spread, top concerns and positives, and notes sorted by helpfulness.
- 👍 **Helpful votes and reporting** on notes, with automatic hiding of notes reported by several people.
- 🔎 **Filters**: time of day, rated by (women / men / non-binary), recency, concern tags, first-hand only, hide low-confidence areas.
- 🔗 **Share** areas, and open them in **Apple Maps** or **Google Maps**.
- 🆘 **SOS tab**: call emergency services, share your location, find nearby police, hospitals, fire stations and pharmacies.
- 🔒 **Privacy by design**: no account, optional on-device profile, anonymous posting, delete all your data.
