# Release notes

## Version 1.2.0 (build 3) — Safer routes
*October 2026*

### New
- 🧭 **Safer walking routes** — get walking routes to any pin, search result or area, ranked by how safe the community says the streets along them feel. The route line is coloured red → green block by block, with **Safest** and **Fastest** badges and a breakdown of how much of each route passes through low-rated areas.
- 🔒 **Walk with me on your Lock Screen** — a Live Activity shows your countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with **I'm safe** and **+10 min** buttons. It turns into an “Overdue” reminder if you don't check in.
- 🗣️ **Siri, Shortcuts & the Action button** — “Hey Siri, start Walk with me in How Safe”, “I'm safe in How Safe”, “Alert my contacts with How Safe”. Add any of them to the Action button.
- One tap from a route starts **Walk with me** with a check-in time based on the walk.

### Privacy & safety
- Routes are calculated by Apple Maps; safety scoring happens on your iPhone. Your trips aren't stored or uploaded.

## Version 1.1.0 (build 2) — Stay safe on the move
*October 2026*

### New
- 🚶 **Walk with me** — set a check-in timer for your trip and pick trusted contacts. If you don't tap **I'm safe** in time, How Safe reminds you, then opens a text to your contacts with your location, ready to send. A countdown banner stays on the map while you walk.
- 🔔 **Area alerts** — optional notifications when you walk into an area the community rated unsafe, after dark only if you prefer. Runs entirely on your iPhone.
- 🖼️ **Share with a map** — sharing an area now includes a map image showing the area's colour and rating.
- ➕➖ **Zoom buttons** on the map.

### Improved
- The SOS tab is now the **Safety** tab, bringing together emergency calling, Walk with me, area alerts and nearby safe places.

### Privacy & safety
- Trusted contacts are stored only on your iPhone. How Safe never sends messages on its own — you always tap Send.
- Area alerts and Walk with me can use your location in the background; it is processed on-device and never uploaded. See the updated [privacy policy](../privacy-policy/).

## Version 1.0.0 (build 1): first release
*October 2026*

### New
- 🗺️ **Community safety map**: Apple Maps with a red → amber → green overlay. Colour strength shows how many people rated an area.
- ➕ **Rate any spot**: five safety levels, time of day, weekday or weekend, place type, 24 concern and positive tags, alone or with others, first-hand or second-hand, plus an optional note.
- 📍 **Area details**: day vs after-dark scores, how ratings are spread, top concerns and positives, and notes sorted by helpfulness.
- 👍 **Helpful votes and reporting** on notes, with automatic hiding of notes reported by several people.
- 🔎 **Filters**: time of day, rated by (women / men / non-binary), recency, concern tags, first-hand only, hide low-confidence areas.
- 🔗 **Share** areas, and open them in **Apple Maps** or **Google Maps**.
- 🆘 **SOS tab**: call emergency services, share your location, find nearby police, hospitals, fire stations and pharmacies.
- 🔒 **Privacy by design**: no account, optional on-device profile, anonymous posting, delete all your data.
