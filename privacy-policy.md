# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 9, 2026 · **Version:** 1.0

How Safe ("the app", "we") is built to help people feel safer, and that includes being safe with your data.
This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version
- No account, no name, no email, no phone number.
- No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. We don't sell or share data with data brokers.
- Your profile details are **optional**, stored **only on your device**, and attached to ratings only if you switch that on.
- Your **location history is never stored**. Ratings you post are public and anonymous.
- You can delete everything you've posted at any time.

## 1. What we collect

### 1.1 Information you choose to post (public)
When you post a rating, these details are stored in the app's public database (Apple CloudKit) and anyone using the app can see them:

| Data | Details |
|---|---|
| Location of the rated spot | Rounded to about 11 m. The spot you pick, which isn't necessarily where you are. |
| Safety level, tags, time of day, day type, place type, company (alone or in a group), source (first-hand or second-hand) | What you selected |
| Note | Optional free text, up to 500 characters |
| Date and time posted | |
| Gender, age range, travel mode | **Only if** you turned on "Attach these to my ratings". Coarse categories only; never your exact age. |
| An anonymous author ID | A random, app-specific identifier from Apple's CloudKit. Other users can't see it, and we can't use it to find your Apple ID, name or email. |

### 1.2 Votes and reports
When you mark a note helpful or not helpful, or report it, we store the vote or report, the note it applies to, and your anonymous ID. This is so each person counts only once.

### 1.3 Information that stays on your device
- Your profile (gender, age range, travel mode, sharing preference)
- Whether you've completed onboarding and accepted the guidelines
- A count of recent submissions, used to limit spam

### 1.4 Location
With your permission ("While Using the App"), the app uses your current location to centre the map,
suggest a spot to rate, and find nearby safe places on the SOS tab. Your location is **processed on your
device and never uploaded or stored**, except for the rounded location of a rating you choose to post.

### 1.5 What we don't collect
Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise age, browsing history, advertising
identifier, or a continuous location trail.

## 2. How we use it
- To show the community safety map and area details
- To calculate area scores, as described in [How scores work](../how-scores-work/)
- To prevent spam and abuse (one vote per person, rate limits, reports)
- To moderate content that breaks the [Community guidelines](../community-guidelines/)

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

## 3. Where data is stored
Public ratings, votes and reports are stored in **Apple CloudKit** (Apple's iCloud servers) under the
app's container. Apple handles them under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
Posting needs you to be signed in to iCloud on your device, but your Apple ID isn't shared with us or with other users.

When you open a place in **Apple Maps** or **Google Maps**, or use search, the coordinates are sent to
that service under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Sharing
We don't sell, rent or trade personal data. Public ratings can be seen by every app user. We may disclose
information if the law requires it, for example a valid court order. Because ratings are anonymous, we
generally hold no information that identifies you.

## 5. Retention
Ratings stay visible until you delete them or until they're removed for breaking the guidelines. Older
ratings count for less in the score over time (see [How scores work](../how-scores-work/)).

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **Edit your profile, or stop attaching it:** Settings → Profile & privacy
- **Delete a single rating:** My Ratings → swipe left → Delete
- **Delete everything you've posted, voted on or reported:** Settings → Your data → Delete all my ratings, votes & reports
- **Location:** iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → How Safe
- Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct,
  delete or move your data. Most of these you can do directly in the app; for anything else, contact us (below).

## 7. Children
How Safe is not intended for anyone under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and the age ranges
in the app start at 18.

## 8. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections, and on-device data uses iOS data protection. No system is
perfectly secure, so please don't post anything sensitive in notes.

## 9. Changes
If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date, note the change in the
[release notes](../release-notes/), and ask you to review it in the app if the change is significant.

## 10. Contact
Open an issue at [github.com/giridharmb/How-Safe-Docs/issues](https://github.com/giridharmb/How-Safe-Docs/issues)
using the **Privacy request** template. For private requests, write "please contact me privately" and we'll reply with a private channel.
