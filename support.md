# Help & FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
No. You can browse the map without one. To post, you need to be signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, but your Apple ID isn't shown to anyone.

**Why is an area faint or uncoloured?**
It has few or no ratings. Add one!

**Why did my rating not change the colour much?**
Each person counts once per cell, and the score blends everyone's ratings, weighted by recency. See [How scores work](../how-scores-work/).

**Can I change or delete my rating?**
Rate the place again to replace your rating, or go to **My Ratings** and swipe left to delete it.

**What does "Rated by: women" do?**
It shows scores calculated only from people who chose to share that they're women. Sharing is optional, so these scores use fewer ratings.

**How do I report a harmful note?**
Tap **⋯** on the note and choose **Report this note**.

**How do I delete all my data?**
Settings → Your data → Delete all my ratings, votes & reports.

**Does Walk with me text my contacts automatically?**
No. iOS doesn't allow apps to send texts silently. When your timer runs out you get notifications, and
How Safe opens a ready-to-send message — you (or anyone with your phone) tap Send. For continuous live
location, also share your location with them in Find My.

**Why does Area alerts ask for "Always" location access?**
So iOS can wake How Safe when you enter a watched area while the app is closed. Your location never
leaves your phone.

**How is the “Safest” route chosen?**
Apple Maps suggests walking routes (plus a couple of detours we ask for). How Safe checks every ~20 m of
each route against community ratings and picks the one that spends the least distance in low-rated
areas. If nobody has rated the streets yet, no route is marked safest. See [How scores work](../how-scores-work/).

**Which Siri phrases work?**
“Start Walk with me in How Safe”, “I'm safe in How Safe”, “Add time to my walk in How Safe”,
“Alert my contacts with How Safe” and “How long is left on my walk in How Safe”. They also appear in the
Shortcuts app and can be assigned to the Action button (Settings → Action Button → Shortcut).

**Is the emergency call button a replacement for emergency services?**
No. It just starts a call to your local emergency number after you confirm.
