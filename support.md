# Help & FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
No. You can browse the map without one. To post, you need to be signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, but your Apple ID isn't shown to anyone.

**Why is an area faint or uncoloured?**
It has few or no ratings. Add one!

**Why did my rating not change the colour much?**
Each person counts once per cell, and the score blends everyone's ratings, weighted by recency. See [How scores work](../how-scores-work/).

**Can I change or delete my rating?**
Rate the place again to replace your rating, or go to **My Ratings** and swipe left to delete it.

**What does "Rated by: women" do?**
It shows scores calculated only from people who chose to share that they're women. Sharing is optional, so these scores use fewer ratings.

**How do I report a harmful note?**
Tap **⋯** on the note and choose **Report this note**.

**How do I delete all my data?**
Settings → Your data → Delete all my ratings, votes & reports.

**Is the SOS button a replacement for emergency services?**
No. It just starts a call to your local emergency number after you confirm.
