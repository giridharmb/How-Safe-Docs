## Description (4000)
How Safe is a community map of how safe places feel.

People rate streets, parks, stations, parking lots and nightlife spots from Very unsafe to Very safe. The map turns their ratings into colour: red where people feel unsafe, amber where they say to stay alert, and green where they feel comfortable. The more people agree, the stronger the colour.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
Whether you're walking home after a late shift, visiting a new city, choosing where to wait for a ride, or picking a park for the kids, How Safe helps you decide with real experiences from your community.

SEE WHAT MATTERS TO YOU
• Separate daytime and after-dark scores
• Filter by who rated, e.g. only women
• Filter by concerns like poor lighting, harassment or isolated areas
• Show only recent or first-hand experiences

RATE IN SECONDS
• Tap anywhere on the map
• Choose a safety level, the time, and the type of place
• Add tags: well lit, busy, CCTV, harassment, being followed, theft and more
• Add an optional note to help others

TRUSTWORTHY BY DESIGN
• One person, one voice: your newest rating replaces your old one
• Recent ratings count more
• Vote notes helpful, and report anything harmful
• Transparent scoring, published in full

SHARE AND GO
• Share an area with friends and family
• Open any spot in Apple Maps or Google Maps

SAFER ROUTES
• Walking routes coloured block by block by community safety ratings
• See which route is Safest and which is Fastest

WALK WITH ME
• Countdown on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
• “Hey Siri, I'm safe in How Safe” — or put it on the Action button
• Set a check-in timer for your trip and choose trusted contacts
• Miss your check-in and How Safe opens a text with your location, ready to send
• Live countdown on the map

AREA ALERTS
• Optional heads-up when you walk into an area the community rated unsafe
• After-dark only, if you prefer — all processed on your iPhone

SAFETY TAB
• Call your local emergency number in one tap
• Share your current location
• Find the nearest police stations, hospitals and pharmacies

PRIVACY FIRST
• No sign-up, no ads, no trackers
• Profile details are optional and stay on your phone
• Your location history is never stored
• Delete everything you've posted at any time

How Safe shows community impressions, not official crime statistics. In an emergency, always call your local emergency number.
