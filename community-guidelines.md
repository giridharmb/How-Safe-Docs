# Community Guidelines

How Safe only works if ratings are honest, respectful and focused on **places**.

## ✅ Do
- **Rate public places:** streets, parks, stations, parking lots, venues.
- **Describe what you noticed:** lighting, crowds, incidents, how it felt, and what time it was.
- **Be specific and fair.** "Underpass has no lights after 9pm" helps people. "This area is bad" doesn't.
- **Mark second-hand information** as "Heard from someone".
- **Update your rating** if things change. Your newest rating replaces your older one in the score.
- **Vote helpful** on notes that are clear and useful.

## 🚫 Don't
- **Identify people.** No names, photos, phone numbers, licence plates, social handles, or descriptions that could single out someone.
- **Rate private homes** or point to where a specific person lives or works.
- **Discriminate.** Don't rate an area based on the race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, income, disability, sexual orientation or gender identity of the people who live or gather there. Rate behaviour and conditions, not groups.
- **Use hate speech, threats or harassment.**
- **Manipulate scores** by brigading, posting the same rating repeatedly, or using several devices to rate one place.
- **Advertise or spam.**
- **Make up incidents.**

## What happens to content that breaks the rules
- Notes that several people report are **hidden automatically** until they're reviewed.
- Moderators may remove content and restrict repeat offenders.
- If you think something was removed by mistake, open an issue using the **Content report / appeal** template.

## Emergencies
How Safe isn't a way to report crimes. If you see a crime or someone is in danger, contact local emergency services.
